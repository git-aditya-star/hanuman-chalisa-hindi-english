श्री हनुमान चालीसा

Hindi verses with line-by-line English translation

दोहा — Opening Couplets

श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज, निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।

With the dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I first cleanse the mirror of my mind.

बरनऊं रघुबर बिमल जसु, जो दायकु फल चारि।।

Then I describe the flawless glory of Raghubar (Rama), who grants the four fruits of life.

बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके, सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार।

Knowing my body to be without wisdom, I remember you, O Son of the Wind.

बल बुद्धि बिद्या देहु मोहिं, हरहु कलेस बिकार।।

Grant me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and remove my afflictions and flaws.

चौपाई — Forty Verses

1

जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर।

Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue.

जय कपीस तिहुं लोक उजागर।।

Victory to the lord of monkeys, who illumines all three worlds.

2

राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा।

Messenger of Rama, abode of matchless strength.

अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा।।

Known as the son of Anjani and the son of the Wind.

3

महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी।

Great hero, mighty and thunderbolt-limbed.

कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी।।

Remover of evil thoughts, companion of good sense.

4

कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा।

Golden-hued, adorned in beautiful attire.

कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा।।

Earrings in your ears, curly locks of hair.

5

हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजे।

A mace and a banner shine in your hands.

कांधे मूंज जनेऊ साजे।।

A sacred thread of munja grass adorns your shoulder.

6

संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन।

Incarnation of Shiva, son of Kesari.

तेज प्रताप महा जग बंदन।।

Your glory and might are worshipped by the whole world.

7

बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर।

Learned, virtuous, and exceedingly clever.

राम काज करिबे को आतुर।।

Ever eager to carry out Rama's tasks.

8

प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया।

You delight in hearing the Lord's deeds.

राम लखन सीता मन बसिया।।

Rama, Lakshman and Sita dwell in your heart.

9

सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा।

Taking a tiny form, you showed yourself to Sita.

बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा।।

Taking a fearsome form, you burned down Lanka.

10

भीम रूप धरि असुर सँहारे।

Taking a huge form, you destroyed the demons.

रामचंद्र के काज सँवारे।।

And accomplished the tasks of Ramachandra.

11

लाय सजीवन लखन जियाये।

Bringing the life-giving herb, you revived Lakshman.

श्रीरघुबीर हरषि उर लाये।।

Sri Raghubir joyfully embraced you.

12

रघुपति कीन्ही बहुत बड़ाई।

The Lord of the Raghus praised you greatly.

तुम मम प्रिय भरतहि सम भाई।।

"You are as dear to me as my brother Bharat."

13

सहस बदन तुम्हरो जस गावैं।

The thousand-headed serpent sings your glory.

अस कहि श्रीपति कंठ लगावैं।।

Saying this, the Lord of Sri embraces you.

14

सनकादिक ब्रह्मादि मुनीसा।

Sanaka and the other sages, Brahma and the great seers.

नारद सारद सहित अहीसा।।

Narada, Sarasvati, and the lord of serpents too.

15

जम कुबेर दिगपाल जहां ते।

Yama, Kubera, and the guardians of the directions.

कबि कोबिद कहि सके कहां ते।।

Even poets and scholars cannot fully describe your glory.

16

तुम उपकार सुग्रीवहिं कीन्हा।

You did a great favor for Sugriva.

राम मिलाय राज पद दीन्हा।।

Uniting him with Rama, you gave him the kingdom.

17

तुम्हरो मंत्र बिभीषन माना।

Vibhishan accepted your counsel.

लंकेश्वर भए सब जग जाना।।

And became the lord of Lanka — the whole world knows this.

18

जुग सहस्र जोजन पर भानू।

The sun, thousands of yojanas away.

लील्यो ताहि मधुर फल जानू।।

You swallowed it, thinking it a sweet fruit.

19

प्रभु मुद्रिका मेलि मुख माहीं।

Placing the Lord's ring in your mouth.

जलधि लांघि गये अचरज नाहीं।।

You crossed the ocean — no wonder at all for you.

20

दुर्गम काज जगत के जेते।

Whatever difficult tasks exist in this world.

सुगम अनुग्रह तुम्हरे तेते।।

Become easy by your grace alone.

21

राम दुआरे तुम रखवारे।

You are the guard at Rama's door.

होत न आज्ञा बिनु पैसारे।।

No one enters without your permission.

22

सब सुख लहै तुम्हारी सरना।

All happiness is found in your shelter.

तुम रक्षक काहू को डरना।।

With you as protector, there is nothing to fear.

23

आपन तेज सम्हारो आपै।

You alone can contain your own power.

तीनों लोक हांक तें कांपै।।

The three worlds tremble at your roar.

24

भूत पिसाच निकट नहिं आवै।

Ghosts and evil spirits dare not come near.

महाबीर जब नाम सुनावै।।

When the name of the great hero is spoken.

25

नासै रोग हरै सब पीरा।

Diseases vanish, all pain is removed.

जपत निरंतर हनुमत बीरा।।

For one who constantly chants the name of brave Hanuman.

26

संकट तें हनुमान छुड़ावै।

Hanuman delivers one from troubles.

मन क्रम बचन ध्यान जो लावै।।

Who fixes their thought, deed and word on him.

27

सब पर राम तपस्वी राजा।

Above all reigns Rama, the ascetic king.

तिन के काज सकल तुम साजा।।

And you accomplish all of his tasks.

28

और मनोरथ जो कोई लावै।

And whoever brings any other wish to you.

सोइ अमित जीवन फल पावै।।

They obtain the boundless fruit of life.

29

चारों जुग परताप तुम्हारा।

Your glory spans all four ages.

है परसिद्ध जगत उजियारा।।

It is renowned and illumines the whole world.

30

साधु संत के तुम रखवारे।

You are the protector of saints and sages.

असुर निकंदन राम दुलारे।।

Destroyer of demons, beloved of Rama.

31

अष्ट सिद्धि नौ निधि के दाता।

You are the giver of the eight siddhis and nine treasures.

अस बर दीन जानकी माता।।

This boon was granted to you by Mother Janaki (Sita).

32

राम रसायन तुम्हरे पासा।

The elixir of devotion to Rama is with you.

सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा।।

May you remain forever the servant of the Lord of the Raghus.

33

तुम्हरे भजन राम को पावै।

Through devotion to you, one attains Rama.

जनम जनम के दुख बिसरावै।।

And forgets the sorrows of countless lifetimes.

34

अंतकाल रघुबर पुर जाई।

At the final hour, one goes to the abode of Raghubar.

जहां जन्म हरिभक्त कहाई।।

And wherever born, is known as a devotee of Hari.

35

और देवता चित्त न धरई।

One need not hold any other deity in mind.

हनुमत सेइ सर्ब सुख करई।।

Serving Hanuman alone brings all happiness.

36

संकट हरै मिटै सब पीरा।

Troubles are removed, all pain is erased.

जो सुमिरै हनुमत बल बीरा।।

For one who remembers mighty, valiant Hanuman.

37

जै जै जै हनुमान गोसाई।

Victory, victory, victory to you, Lord Hanuman.

कृपा करहु गुरुदेव की नाई।।

Bestow your grace as a true Gurudev would.

38

जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई।

Whoever recites this a hundred times.

छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई।।

Is freed from bondage and attains great happiness.

39

जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चालीसा।

Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa.

होय सिद्धि साखी गौरीसा।।

Attains success — Lord Shiva himself is witness.

40

तुलसीदास सदा हरि चेरा।

Tulsidas is forever the servant of Hari.

कीजै नाथ हृदय महं डेरा।।

O Lord, please make your dwelling in my heart.

दोहा — Closing Couplet

पवनतनय संकट हरन, मंगल मूरति रूप।

O son of the Wind, remover of troubles, embodiment of auspiciousness.

राम लखन सीता सहित, हृदय बसहु सुर भूप।।

Along with Rama, Lakshman and Sita, dwell in my heart, O king among gods.

Traditional text authored by Tulsidas · English lines are original translations for study.
